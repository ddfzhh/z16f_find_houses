# Plan: Finding a House in Redwood City

## First principles

The goal is not "find listings" — listings are easy to find. The goal is
**signing a lease on a place both of us are happy in, at a price we can
sustain, before someone else takes it.** Three things actually decide the
outcome:

1. **Agreement between us.** Two people with unspoken differences in budget
   or priorities will hesitate on a good listing and lose it. Align first.
2. **Speed.** In Redwood City, good houses rent in days. The winner is
   usually whoever tours first and submits a complete application the same day.
3. **Filtering.** Most listings are wrong for us. A clear scorecard lets us
   reject quickly and act decisively on the right one.

So the plan front-loads alignment and preparation, then runs a fast, daily
search loop.

---

## Phase 0 — Align (1–2 days, do together)

Sit down together and fill in the TBD rows in `CLAUDE.md`. Specifically:

- **Total budget and how we split it.** Equal split, or by room size /
  bathroom? (Common: the person with the bigger room or private bath pays more.)
  Rule of thumb: rent ≤ ~30–35% of each person's gross income — landlords
  often require combined income ≥ 2.5–3× rent.
- **Move-in window.** Start searching seriously **30–45 days** before the
  target date; most listings are for immediate or next-month move-in.
- **The 3rd person question.** Is a third roommate planned, or is the extra
  room a guest room / office? This decides 2BR vs 3BR and whether the
  lease needs three names.
- **Commutes.** Where does each of us work/study, how often, and by what
  (car, Caltrain, bike)? Set a max commute time each.
- **Must-haves vs. nice-to-haves.** Use the scorecard below; agree on it
  before touring so we judge listings the same way.

### Scorecard (agree on weights together)

| Criterion | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Within budget (incl. utilities) | Must | Ask what utilities are included |
| ≥ 2 bedrooms (3 if needed) | Must | Rooms roughly equal size if splitting evenly |
| Commute within limit for both | Must | Test at real commute hours |
| 2 bathrooms | Strong want | Big quality-of-life factor for roommates |
| In-unit laundry | Strong want | |
| Parking (garage/driveway) | Depends | Street parking varies a lot by block |
| Yard / outdoor space | Nice | |
| Walkable to downtown / Caltrain | Nice | |
| Pets allowed | Depends | |
| Quiet street, feels safe | Must | Visit at night once before signing |

## Phase 1 — Prepare (2–3 days, in parallel with Phase 0)

Have everything ready so we can apply within hours of a tour.

**Application packet (each person):**
- Photo ID
- Proof of income: last 2–3 pay stubs or offer letter; tax return if self-employed
- Recent bank statements (1–2 months)
- Credit report (free at annualcreditreport.com) — know your score in advance
- References: previous landlord(s) + employer contact
- Short intro paragraph about the two of us (jobs, quiet, non-smokers, etc.)

**Money ready:**
- Security deposit + first month's rent available on short notice.
  California generally caps deposits at **1 month's rent** (2 months for
  some small landlords) — verify for each listing.
- Application/screening fees are capped by California law (around $60
  per adult); never pay one before seeing the place.

**Roommate basics (write down, don't need to be formal):**
- Both names on the lease (we are jointly responsible for full rent)
- How we split rent, utilities, internet, shared supplies
- What happens if one person needs to leave early

## Phase 2 — Learn the area (first week, light effort)

Redwood City neighborhoods, roughly:

| Area | Character | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Downtown / Centennial / Stambaugh-Heller | Walkable, restaurants, Caltrain | Car-light living, transit commute |
| Mount Carmel / Edgewood Park / Eagle Hill | Older single-family houses near downtown | Classic house + walkability |
| Woodside Plaza / Farm Hill / Emerald Hills | Quieter, hilly, west side, near I-280 | Houses with yards, driving commute south/north on 280 |
| Redwood Shores | Planned community by the Bay, newer, pricier | Commutes to Shores offices; far from Caltrain |
| Friendly Acres / Redwood Village / Palm Park | Flat, residential, more affordable | Value; check block by block |
| North Fair Oaks (Middlefield area) | Most affordable, mixed | Budget-first; visit before deciding |

Action: each of us drives/walks 2–3 candidate areas once on a weekday
evening and once on a weekend. Narrow to **2–4 target neighborhoods**.

## Phase 3 — Search loop (daily, until signed)

**Sources (set saved-search alerts on all of these):**
- Zillow, Redfin, Zumper / PadMapper, HotPads, Apartments.com
- Craigslist (SF Bay Area → Peninsula → apts/housing)
- Facebook Marketplace and local housing groups; Nextdoor
- Local property-management company websites (many list houses there first)
- Walking/driving target streets for "For Rent" signs (owner-rented houses
  often never hit the big sites)

**Alert filters:** Redwood City (+ Emerald Hills/North Fair Oaks if open to it),
houses/townhouses, ≥ 2 beds, max price = our budget + ~5%.

**Daily routine (15–20 min):**
1. Check alerts, add every plausible listing to the web app (the agent does this automatically once it runs; see `SPEC.md`).
2. Score it against the scorecard. Reject fast.
3. For anything that passes: message/call **the same day** to book a tour.
   Mention both of us, combined income, move-in date, and that we can
   apply immediately.

**Tours:**
- Ideally both of us attend; if not, the one who goes takes video.
- Checklist: water pressure, cell signal, noise, heating, parking, laundry,
  signs of mold/pests, room sizes, storage, what utilities are included,
  pet/guest rules, lease length, who handles maintenance.
- If it's a yes, **apply the same day**.

---

## Phase 4 — Decide and sign

- Read the full lease before signing. Check: rent, deposit, lease term,
  renewal terms, utilities, maintenance responsibilities, guest/subletting
  rules (important if a 3rd person may join), early-termination terms.
- Note: many single-family houses owned by individuals are exempt from
  California's statewide rent cap (AB 1482) — ask what happens to rent at
  renewal.
- Do a move-in inspection with photos/video of every room; send it to the
  landlord in writing.

---

## Red flags (walk away)

- Asks for deposit/fees before you've seen the inside
- "Owner is out of the country, I'll mail you the keys"
- Price far below the market reference in `CLAUDE.md`
- Payment by wire, Zelle to a stranger, gift cards, or crypto
- Won't let you meet in person or verify they own/manage the property
